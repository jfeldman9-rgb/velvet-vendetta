VELVET VENDETTA
Nightshade Arcade

GET INTO THE RING

Extract the entire ZIP to a folder on your computer. Open index.html from the
extracted folder in a recent desktop browser. Click Start Arcade, choose Cory
or Bob, then click Fight. Choose your arena on the title screen before starting.
For phones, use the hosted game link. The browser edition includes touch controls.

The first click enables sound. The sound button mutes or restores the music,
voices and effects. This download plays the three scores as lossless 48 kHz,
24-bit stereo WAV files. Menu, opera and glasshouse themes, character lines
and combat effects were rebuilt with ElevenLabs for this edition. All art,
fonts, audio and game code are local.

HOW TO PLAY

A / D or Left / Right     Walk
W or Up                   Jump
S or Down                 Crouch
Shift or I                Hold guard
J                         Jab
K                         Heavy kick
L                         Signature attack, costs 40 meter
F                         Trigger an eggplant fatality when prompted
Esc or P                  Pause

Win two rounds to take the match. Each round lasts 60 game seconds. The fighter
with more health wins at time. Tied health triggers a one-hit sudden death.
Jabs are quick. Kicks have more reach and take longer to recover. A crouch
can duck a standing jab. Land hits or block attacks to build signature meter.

Cory wears gold-trimmed plum couture. Orchid Rush sends a burst of petals
across the stage. Bob wears a black agent suit and shades. Null Protocol
releases a shorter, quicker digital pulse.

EGGPLANT FATALITIES

After the deciding round, press F or tap Perform Fatality before the countdown
ends. Cory performs Aubergine Ovation. Bob executes Protocol Violet. Allow
the countdown to expire for an ordinary victory.

To see either sequence immediately, pick Training, choose the fighter, enter
the arena, then select Pause and Rehearse Fatality. Practice resumes afterward.
